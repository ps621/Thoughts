## Strategic reads
1. **Money and travel are the two big categories outside care.** In India, "senior citizen FD rates" is a query so large that publishers compete for it every month. Pilgrimage travel is the Indian version of the Western "50+ trip".
2. **In India, YouTube and WhatsApp matter more than Google for this audience.** Distribution belongs on YouTube and WhatsApp, not only on search.
3. **Scam fear is a way in to trust.** Digital-arrest and cyber-safety content reaches both seniors and their worried children, and gives a care brand a reason to be in touch that isn't a sale.
4. **Midlife is shifting from "crisis" to "reset".** The 45–60 buyer is looking up longevity, strength training and menopause. That is your future customer, and the payer for their parents today.
5. **Wills, nomination and power of attorney** are high-intent searches made by children on behalf of parents. There's little good Indian content and they fit the "family admin" role well.
6. **Bundle ideas:** safe pilgrimage (travel + companion + medical cover); a digital-safety workshop that leads into a care plan; a "parents' money health check" (FDs, insurance, will).
