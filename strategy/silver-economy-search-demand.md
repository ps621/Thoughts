# Search demand: silver economy and the middle-aged (India + global)

_Drafted 2026-09-24. First pass from published sources. It is not keyword-tool data._

## Caveat first
Public reports rarely give search terms broken down by age. What's below:
- **[S]**: stated in a published source (links at bottom)
- **[I]**: inferred from category knowledge. Check it in Google Keyword Planner or Trends before betting budget on it.

## 1. Global: seniors and families searching for care
| Theme | Terms | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Home over institution | "home care", "in-home care", "home care near me" | [S] "home care" has ~2x the volume of "nursing home", has grown steadily for 10 years, and is outpacing "assisted living" |
| Housing | "assisted living", "senior living", "nursing home" | [S] "assisted living" is the top long-term-care housing term; "senior living" is rising and has passed "nursing home", which is declining |
| Condition-led | "Alzheimer's", "dementia care", "memory care" | [S] "Alzheimer's" and "senior care" run at ~5x "elder care" and "care manager" |
| Vocabulary | "senior care" >> "elder care" / "aging care" / "geriatric care manager" | [S] People search in plain words, not industry jargon |
| Tech | "fall detection", "medical alert", "GPS tracker for elderly", "remote monitoring" | [I], backed by market growth in [S] |

| Own health | diabetes, BP, thyroid, heart attack at young age | [S] heart-attack searches now include people in their 20s and 30s |
| Health insurance | "health insurance for parents", "senior citizen health insurance" | [I]. This is one of the highest-intent terms in the category |

## Strategic reads
1. **Home beats institution** in both India and the West. Position around care *at home*.
2. **Search vocabulary splits by market**: the West says "home care / senior living"; India says "nurse at home / attendant / old age home". Write copy and keywords in the user's words.
3. **The buyer is often the child, not the senior**: middle-aged or NRI children searching on behalf of parents. Target the payer.
4. **Diabetes and heart health lead health searches in India.** Chronic-condition management is a way in to longer care relationships.
5. **Money questions are high-intent**: insurance for parents, FD rates, pensions. Content here builds trust with the payer.
